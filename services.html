Our Services

At Imperial Builders, we provide a comprehensive range of construction services tailored to your needs. From transforming basements into legal, income-generating suites to crafting luxury interiors, our expertise ensures every project meets the highest standards of quality and compliance.

Legal Basement Suites & Secondary Units

Transform your basement into a compliant, income-generating space. We manage permits, inspections, and every detail from architectural plans to final finishes—ensuring full adherence to Ontario Building Code standards.

Energy-Efficient Insulation

Reduce energy costs and enhance comfort with our insulation solutions. Choose from spray foam, fiberglass, or eco-friendly options that provide excellent moisture control, soundproofing, and energy efficiency.

Precision Framing & Structural Solutions

Our expert framing services include new basement framing, load-bearing wall modifications, custom staircases, and engineered solutions for commercial projects—delivered with licensed expertise.

Drywall Installation & Finishing

Experience flawless drywall installation using dust-free, HEPA-filtered processes. We offer patchwork, skim coating, and complete level 5 finishes for mirror-smooth, durable walls.

Luxury Flooring & Tiling Solutions

Upgrade your space with premium flooring options including hardwood, porcelain, luxury vinyl, and eco-friendly materials. Enjoy bespoke installations with heated floors, custom patterns, and waterproof finishes.

Interior Finishing & Custom Designs

From custom trim and crown molding to turnkey interior finishing, our team brings your vision to life with modern, eco-friendly designs that integrate smart home technology and add value to your property.
